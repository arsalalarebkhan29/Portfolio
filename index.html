A dedicated educator and literary scholar, Arsala Khan brings both academic excellence and creative depth to her teaching.

At just 26, she has achieved a remarkable 99.7 percentile in UGC NET 2025, holds a Master's in English Literature (80.7%, First Division with Distinction) from Rani Durgavati Vishwavidyalaya, and currently serves as an Assistant Professor — teaching both B.A. and M.A. students with clarity, passion and purpose.

Her teaching philosophy is rooted in making language accessible — from university-level literary theory down to early childhood phonics. A certified Jolly Phonics Instructor, she bridges formal academia with foundational learning.
